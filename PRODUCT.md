# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Payment operations and reliability teams who need to notice conversion degradation quickly, isolate the affected transaction segment, understand the evidence, and decide on a response while incidents are still unfolding.

## Product Purpose

Control Tower monitors a simulated payment stream, detects meaningful conversion drops, isolates likely root causes across payment dimensions, explains each diagnosis with traceable evidence and financial impact, and recommends an action. It is an observability and decision-support surface; it never executes remediation.

## Brand Personality

Calm, precise, operational. The interface should feel trustworthy under pressure, make dense financial data easy to scan, and keep the system's reasoning inspectable rather than theatrical.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-style dashboards, decorative analytics, neon or gaming aesthetics, oversized cards, heavy gradients, glass effects, playful controls, excessive motion, and any visual treatment that competes with incident severity or evidence.

## Design Principles

1. Surface urgency without creating panic.
2. Keep the path from symptom to evidence visible and easy to scan.
3. Preserve familiar operational patterns so users can act without relearning controls.
4. Use color only when it communicates selection, health, severity, or action.
5. Keep every recommendation visibly separate from execution.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA contrast and keyboard access for primary navigation and controls. Never rely on color alone for status, preserve visible focus states, respect reduced-motion preferences, and keep dense operational text readable without shrinking it to fit.
